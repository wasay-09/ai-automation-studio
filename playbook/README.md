## The one-page version

1. **Lead with a demo, never a CV.** Every message links to a live demo, not to a résumé. The demos need no sign-up for exactly this reason — friction kills the click.
2. **Sell an outcome, not a technology.** Nobody buys "RAG pipeline". They buy "every after-hours enquiry gets answered".
3. **Free audit, then fixed-price pilot.** The audit is a real deliverable, not a sales call in disguise. It's also the cheapest way to prove you're competent before anyone risks money.
4. **Fixed scope beats hourly.** Hourly rates get benchmarked against the cheapest freelancer on the platform. Fixed-price outcomes get benchmarked against the cost of the problem.
5. **Niche down after project three.** Whichever vertical pays fastest and complains least — go all in on it. "AI automation for Dubai real-estate brokerages" outsells "AI automation" every time.

## Realistic first-90-days sequence

| Weeks | Focus | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Portfolio live, 40 targeted cold emails/week, daily build-in-public post | 3–5 audit calls |
| 3–4 | Convert audits → 1 paid pilot, even at a discount | 1 paying client |
| 5–8 | Deliver flawlessly, get a written testimonial + case study with real numbers | 1 reference |
| 9–12 | Raise price, use the case study in outreach, convert pilot → retainer | 2–3 clients / recurring |

The first client is the expensive one. Underprice it deliberately, over-deliver, and buy the case study.
