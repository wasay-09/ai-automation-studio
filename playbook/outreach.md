# Outreach — finding and opening offshore clients

## Where the buyers actually are

**UAE / GCC** — the strongest fit for this portfolio. Businesses are service-heavy, lead-driven, and
comfortable paying for speed.

| Segment | Why they buy | Which demo to send |
|---|---|---|
| Real-estate brokerages (Dubai, Abu Dhabi) | Portal leads go cold in minutes; agents drown in junk enquiries | LeadFlow AI |
| Dental / aesthetic / medical clinics | After-hours enquiries lost; reception overloaded | Concierge Agent |
| Accounting & bookkeeping firms | Invoice re-keying is their largest labour cost | DocuFlow |
| Marketing & video agencies | Retainer margins die on manual repurposing | ContentForge |
| B2B services / SaaS | Outbound burning domains, no reply rate | Outreach Engine |

**US** — higher budgets, more competition, expects sharper positioning. Target agencies (they resell
you), PE-backed SMEs mid-consolidation, and B2B SaaS between $1M and $20M ARR.

**Where to find them:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator filters, Google Maps for local service businesses,
Clutch and DesignRush agency lists, Apollo, and the member directories of local chambers of commerce.
For agencies specifically, the white-label angle ("I'm the automation team you resell") converts far
better than pitching them as an end client.

## Cold email — the one that works

Short, one specific observation, one link, one small ask. No attachments, no deck.

**Subject:** `question about your inbound leads`

```
Hi {{first_name}},

I looked at {{company}}'s enquiry form — it goes to a shared inbox, so I'm guessing
first replies depend on whoever happens to be free.

I build systems that score an inbound lead and draft a personalised first reply in
under a minute. Here's one running live, no sign-up:
https://wasay-09.github.io/leadflow-ai/

If speed-to-lead is costing you deals, worth a 15-minute look?

Wasay
```

Why it works: it's specific about *them* in line one, the proof is one click away and requires nothing,
and the ask is 15 minutes rather than "a call".

**Follow-up 2 (day 3)** — different angle, not a bump:

```
{{first_name}} — one number that made me send the first email: most enquiries that
get a reply within 5 minutes convert several times better than ones answered an
hour later. It's usually the cheapest fix in the funnel.

Happy to map out where yours is leaking, free, whether or not we work together.
```

**Follow-up 3 (day 8)** — the breakup, which pulls more replies than any other message:

```
I'll stop here, {{first_name}}. If inbound response time isn't a priority this
quarter, that's a completely fair answer — just tell me and I'll close the loop.

Demo stays up either way: https://wasay-09.github.io/leadflow-ai/
```

## LinkedIn — connection then value

**Connection note (300 char limit):**
```
Hi {{first}} — I build AI automations for {{industry}} teams (lead response, support,
invoice processing). Not pitching; I post live demos and thought they might be useful
to you.
```

**After they accept, wait 2 days, then:**
```
Thanks for connecting. I put a demo live this week that's relevant to {{company}} —
it scores an inbound lead and drafts the first reply in under a minute, running
entirely in the browser: {{link}}

No sign-up, nothing to install. Curious whether the scoring logic matches how your
team actually triages.
```

Ending on a question about *their* process, rather than a request for their time, roughly doubles the
reply rate.

## Build in public — the inbound channel

Post one thing per weekday on LinkedIn and X. The five projects give you weeks of material:

- The mechanism ("here's how the lead scoring actually works, with the numbers")
- The problem ("duplicate invoices get paid twice more often than finance teams admit")
- A short screen recording of a demo doing something satisfying
- A teardown of a real (anonymised) workflow and what you'd automate first
- Lessons from a build — what broke and why

Inbound from this is slower than cold email but converts at a much higher rate and lets you charge more.

## Upwork / freelance platforms

Worth it only for the first two or three reviews, then leave. Rules that matter:

- Apply within 30 minutes of posting — response time dominates the ranking
- First line must reference something specific from their post, never a template opener
- Link the relevant demo in line two
- Quote fixed price with a scoped deliverable, never an hourly rate
- Skip anything with no payment method verified, or "simple task, should take an hour"

**Proposal skeleton:**
```
You need {{restate their problem in one sentence, more precisely than they did}}.

I've built this exact system before — here's one running live, no sign-up: {{link}}

For your case I'd deliver: {{3 concrete bullets}}
Timeline: {{n}} days. Fixed price: ${{x}}.

One question before I start: {{a sharp question that proves domain understanding}}.
```

## Compliance, briefly

Cold B2B email is legal in most markets when you identify yourself, use a real business address, honour
opt-outs immediately, and don't disguise the subject line. The UAE and US both permit it under those
conditions; the EU/UK are stricter and effectively require legitimate-interest justification. Keep
volumes low, warm the domain first, and never send from your primary domain — use a separate sending
domain so a mistake can't take down your real email.
