## Notes for whoever is sending this

**Anchor before you quote.** Section 2 must appear before section 6, always. A price only means
something next to the cost of the alternative.

**Give three options when the budget is unclear.** People who won't buy "the thing" will often buy
"the middle thing":

| | Starter | Recommended | Complete |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | One workflow | One workflow + integrations | Multiple workflows + dashboard |
| Price | {{x}} | {{1.8x}} | {{3x}} |
| Timeline | {{n}} days | {{n}} days | {{n}} days |

Mark the middle one "recommended". Most people choose it.

**Put an expiry date on it.** Fourteen days, honoured. Proposals without deadlines sit open forever.

**Follow up twice, then stop.** Day 3: "anything in there you'd want changed before deciding?" Day 8:
"closing this one out — should I keep it open?" Then leave it. Chasing past that costs you more in
positioning than the deal is worth.
