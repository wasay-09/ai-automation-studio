03 / FINANCE OPS

DocuFlow

Invoice & receipt processing pipeline
The problem

Bookkeepers re-key hundreds of supplier invoices a month. It's slow, it's expensive, and duplicate invoices quietly get paid twice.

  • PDF in, structured accounting data out — supplier, TRN/VAT, line items, tax, totals
  • Handles UAE (5% VAT, AED), US and UK/EU formats, including their date and number quirks
  • Validates the arithmetic: line items must sum, tax must reconcile, dates must be sane
  • Confidence routing — clean documents auto-approve, only uncertain fields reach a human

Outcome

Roughly four minutes of manual entry per invoice reduced to under twenty seconds of review — with duplicate-payment detection built into the intake.

Questions

The things clients usually ask first

Which timezones do you work across?

I work Gulf Standard Time and overlap the US morning, so Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh and US East Coast teams all get live hours with me. Standups, demos and incident response happen inside your working day, not after it.

Who owns the code?

You do, entirely. Everything is delivered into your repository under a licence you choose, with no runtime dependency on me or on any platform I control. If we stop working together, nothing you rely on stops working.

What does the AI itself cost to run?

That's billed by the model provider directly to your account, and it's usually the smallest line in the budget — most of these systems run in the tens of dollars a month at real volume. Every build routes the cheap deterministic work through plain code and only calls a model where a model genuinely earns its place.

Do these demos need an API key?

No. Every project ships with a demo mode that runs the full pipeline deterministically with no key at all, which is exactly why you can click through all five right now. Point it at a real API key and the same interfaces switch to live model calls.

Can you work with our existing stack?

Yes — these builds already integrate with webhooks, CRMs, Google Sheets, Slack, and the usual sending and scheduling platforms. If you're already running n8n, Make or Zapier, I'll build around it rather than insisting you replace it.

How do we start without committing to anything?

Take the free audit. You get a written map of where the waste is and what it's worth fixing, whether or not you hire me for the build.
